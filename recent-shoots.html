A note on these galleries

Children's faces are blurred in my public galleries. The families here agreed to let their sessions be shown, and obscuring the minors is a precaution I add on top of that — what lives on a public page can be copied, indexed and reused in ways nobody agreed to, and I'd rather that not happen to someone else's kids.

Families receive every image untouched in their private gallery. If you're deciding whether to book and want to see the work as it's actually delivered, ask me and I'll walk you through a full gallery.
