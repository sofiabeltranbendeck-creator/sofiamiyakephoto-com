Recent family sessions

A portfolio shows you ten good frames and tells you nothing about the hour that produced them. These pages show more of each session than a portfolio would, so you can see the setting, the light and how a family actually moved.

Every session below is a family session around Roseville and Sacramento from August and September 2026, newest first. Each one has a page of its own with more of the frames. Read whichever looks closest to your own family. If that sounds like the hour you want, come and say hello.
