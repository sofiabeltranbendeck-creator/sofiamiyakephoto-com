The Story

A January wedding in Santa Clarita

This wedding happened on one of those January days in Santa Clarita when the weather couldn't have been more perfect: clear skies for an outdoor ceremony under a draped wooden pergola, then everyone moved inside for the reception as the evening cooled down.

The two of them were friends for years before they were anything more. They met at church, a long way from where either of them started. She grew up in Argentina, he's originally from Peru, and they found each other here. When he proposed, it was at an aquarium, which still makes me smile.

Their vows were a promise to each other and to God, and the celebration that followed was full of worship music and games, which felt exactly like them.

That's what I wanted these photos to hold: the friendship underneath the vows. The quiet moment with the rings, the portraits by the pond and the lake in soft winter light, the two of them laughing back to back at the reception, and the little details, like the roses, the decorated cookies and the gold bands.

If you're planning a wedding in Sacramento, Roseville or anywhere in California, this is how I'll approach your day too: documenting it as it really happens, with time set aside for the portraits you'll want to keep forever.
