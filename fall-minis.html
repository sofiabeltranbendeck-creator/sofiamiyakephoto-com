Why Fall

A short window, once a year

The Sacramento Valley gets a short run of this every year — dry gold grass, low sun, warm air, no haze. Once it goes, it is gone until next autumn. If you've been meaning to get family photos done, this is the window that makes them look the way you're picturing.

Practical Details

Things people usually ask

Do you come to us, or do we meet somewhere?
Either — whichever you'd rather. Some families want their own backyard or living room; others like meeting somewhere with open evening light. I shoot around Roseville, Rocklin and greater Sacramento, so most spots work. If you'd like me to choose, I have a few favorites.
What does $200 include?
About thirty minutes of shooting at golden hour, and 45 or more edited high-resolution images in a private online gallery you can download, print and share. No hidden session fee on top.
How many people can come?
Mini sessions are built for immediate family — parents and kids. If you're hoping to include grandparents, cousins or a larger group, an extended family session gives everyone the time they need. Just ask and I'll send details.
What should we wear?
I'll send a short styling note once you book. The quick version: coordinate without matching, lean into texture and soft colors, and wear something you actually feel good in. No matching white shirts required.
Can we bring the dog?
Always yes. Dogs make every session better. (Cats are negotiable.)
What if the weather turns?
We'll watch it together and move your session to another evening if we need to — no fee for weather. Fall in the valley is usually reliable, but October has opinions.
How soon do we get the photos?
Your gallery arrives within two weeks of your session. If you need something sooner for a holiday card, tell me when you book and I'll work to it.
