August 2026 · Roseville, California

Nobody standing still

This one ran in bright afternoon light rather than at golden hour, which is not what most people picture for a family session. It worked because this family was never going to sit still anyway, and hard afternoon light suits movement better than it suits posing.

There was a football, a wooden ladder the kids immediately claimed, and a plaid blanket that spent more time being dragged around than sat on. I mostly stayed out of the way and let it happen. One of my favourites from the day is a boy mid-kick with his father turned away in the foreground, completely unaware.

If your kids are the sort who cannot be asked to stand somewhere and smile, this is roughly what a session with us looks like. That is not a problem to be solved. It is the photograph.
