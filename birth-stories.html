Some stories can only be told once

There is no experience quite as unique as the birth of a child. From the maternal strength to the first cry of life, this — out of any other day — you will want to remember.

The Work

Make sure you can cherish every moment, in the now, and forever.

When the time comes, I will be there, as a silent companion. My job will be to document every special moment without disturbing you.

My goal is to capture your baby's first cry, the first touch of sunlight on their face, the quiet intimacy the moment you hold your baby for the first time.

That's what I'm there for. I work silently, with no need for direction. I strive for the genuine shots, making sure these moments transcend the moment.

Practical Details

A Few Things to Know

All birth photography is approached with deep respect for your wishes and boundaries.

When do I book?
As early as possible — I accept a limited number of birth clients each month so I can be fully available when the time comes. Most families book in the second trimester.
What if labor is fast?
I ask for a heads-up as early as possible. If your baby surprises everyone and I don't make it in time, we'll schedule a fresh 48-hour newborn session at no additional charge.
What types of birth do you shoot?
Hospital, birth center, and home births. Vaginal, cesarean, medicated, unmedicated. Every birth is a story worth telling.
How long will you be there?
I arrive during active labor and stay through the first hour postpartum — typically 4 to 10 hours total, though every birth is different. There are no overtime fees if labor runs long.
Do I need to prepare anything in advance?
Nothing at all. I don't need a "photogenic" space or a styled setup — I'm there for what's real. The only thing I ask is that you call or text me as soon as labor begins, even if you think it's early. Just let your care team know you'll have a photographer present; I follow all hospital and birth center protocols and stay entirely out of the way of medical care.
What does a birth session include?
Gallery size depends on the collection you choose — 75+, 150+ or 250+ fully edited, high-resolution images. Your photos stay private by default — I only ever share with your explicit written permission.

Once everyone is home and settled, family sessions pick the story up from there. Still deciding whether you want a photographer in the room? Start with How I work, and the session journal walks through a few recent family sessions frame by frame.
