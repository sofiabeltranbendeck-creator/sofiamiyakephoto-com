This is what your family actually looks like.

Not the version where everyone is still and staring at the camera. The real one — full of noise and movement and the particular way your kids laugh. That one is worth keeping.

The Approach

It's more fun to be genuine

I show up with a plan and a goal to fulfill your vision. I give kids something to do instead of somewhere to stand. I let things get a little chaotic. And somewhere in the middle of that chaos — when your toddler is spinning and your partner is laughing and you've completely forgotten I'm there — I find the photo you're going to print and hang on your wall.

Your family doesn't need to be ready. They just need to be themselves.

Practical Details

Things people usually wonder

When should we book?
Anytime — there's no wrong season for family photos. That said, spring and fall book up quickly. If you have a milestone or holiday in mind, reach out at least 6–8 weeks ahead.
What should we wear?
I'll send you a simple styling guide after you book. The short version: coordinate without matching, lean into texture and layers, and wear something you actually feel good in. No matching white shirts required.
How long are sessions?
Standard family sessions are 60–90 minutes. Extended family or milestone sessions can run up to two hours. I'd rather give you time to settle in than rush.
How many photos will we get?
Galleries typically include 50–80 edited images delivered as high-resolution digital files. You'll have everything you need for prints, frames, albums, and the family group chat.
Do you offer printed products?
Yes — fine art prints, albums, and wall art are available through my print shop. There's nothing like seeing your family on the wall of your own home.
Can we bring the dog?
Always yes. Dogs make every session better. (Cats are negotiable.)
What if the weather doesn't cooperate?
For outdoor sessions, we'll keep an eye on conditions together and reschedule if needed — no fees for weather changes. I have a short list of beautiful indoor backups if you'd like a plan B.
What does a session cost?
Family sessions are $350 for one hour. Extended family sessions are $495 for 90 minutes, and Fresh Family sessions are $425 for one hour, at home or outdoors. Every session includes your edited, high-resolution gallery. If you're not sure which one fits, reach out and I'll help you choose, no pressure.

Let's document this chapter

Sessions book up, especially in spring and fall. If you know you want photos this season, don't wait — let's figure out the details together.
